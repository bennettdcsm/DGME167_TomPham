Cursed Dragons Amulet:

Prologue

The age of dragons ended many millenniums ago. Dragons are mythical creatures, so it's makes sense why flying dragons aren't real. That's what people thought till one day an archologist named Sara Cross uncovered a cursed dragon amulet upon her excavation of the Ancient Fallen Dragon Temple Ruins deep in the mountains of China. She believes it pre-dates back the early years of China during the dynasty eras. But like any archeologists they don't believe in magic. Sara decided to send this amulet to a college of hers back in the states and in Space City that studies ancient artifacts.

But little does she know sending this amulet to a futuristic setting were flying cars, robots, and ships dominate the skies and shops. Would lead to utter Chaos and Destruction of an entire city or worse end a world.

After a long day of digging and research she decided it was time for her to return to her hotel and rest. She still must stay in Shanghai for 10 more days to resume her excavation of artifacts that the Museum back in the states can add to their Chinese History Collection.

Strixhaven Magic Academy:

Prologue

It has been 30 years since Shadow went into hiding. Shadow went into hiding because society mistreated him for being a Drago panther. They played with his wings unaware that they were hurting him. They pulled and tugged on his wings like it was a toy. They even picked on him for being a freak of nature. Other jaguars and leopards also picked on him. Shadow kept to himself most of the time. While in hiding he devoted his time to mastering the acranium. He even made his own personal laser claw gloves. He eventually enhanced his claws to imitate actual laser claws. Shadow's personal claws eventually fused with the laser nanotech due continuous exposure. On that same faithful night, a wild falcon with an injured wing fumbled into his jungle shack.

Although Shadow is very anti-social, he still had some feelings for some wildlife. Shadow tended to the falcon, and it eventually became his new companion. Things were looking up for him till he received an invitation to attend an academy that taught magic and other subjects. This did not sit well with him. But his now pet falcon suggest he gave socialization a second chance. Shadow eventually agreed but he brought defensive gadgets with him. His new adventure outside the jungle was about to begin.
