About Cobalt Panther Studios

Description

My inspiration for the website came from my interest in the depths of the ocean and the far reaches of space. Both are filled with mystery and wonder. I hope visitors find the site both calming and mysterious at the sametime.

The image you see to the right. Is the logo I chose to best represent my website. I chose that because it fits the space like theme.

As you resume your journey throughout Cobalt Panther Studios you'll see how visual art and animations mixed with sound will give one a oceanic to galactic experience. The site will explore both the depths of the ocean and might even take one to the stars.
